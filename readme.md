### 🚗 What I'm working on

I'm the founder of **[Tiravu](https://tiravu.com)** — an app that gets you 24/7 roadside assistance in one tap. No phone trees, no language barriers, no guessing how much it'll cost. Just tap, see the price up front, and watch help drive to you.

We're launching soon in Latvia, and I'm wearing pretty much every hat that fits: product, mobile, backend, web, design decisions, the works. It's the hardest and most rewarding thing I've done — turns out shipping a real product for real people in a real city is a different game than shipping side projects.

If you're curious, drop your email on the [waitlist](https://tiravu.com) or follow Tiravu on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/tiravu/) · [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/tiravuapp/) · [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@tiravuapp).
